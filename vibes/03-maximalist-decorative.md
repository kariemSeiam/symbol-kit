# 03 — Maximalist Decorative

> Illuminated manuscripts, ornamental borders, stars and flourishes. This vibe celebrates the glyph as art.

## Signature

`╭╮╰╯ ❖ ❀ ✦ ★ ⁂`

## Philosophy

Where terminal-hacker is structure and modern-minimal is restraint, maximalist-decorative is abundance. Rounded corners, stars in multiple weights, floral ornaments. Every element is embellished.

This vibe is for celebratory UIs, achievement screens, and any context where delight is the primary goal.

## When to Choose

- Achievement and reward screens
- Holiday or event-themed UIs
- "Easter egg" moments in otherwise minimal apps

## When Not to Choose

- Status dashboards (ornaments obscure data)
- Terminal contexts (many glyphs won't render)
- Diff/code review (ornaments are noise)
